# Critique of the actual current application

Baseline 7cbcb91. Chromium, 1440×1000 and 390×1000. [Render observations](current/RENDER_OBSERVATIONS.json) record actual scroll positions, heading/status geometry and document height. Captures use reduced motion to settle the current UI; this is not a new motion benchmark. No proposed interface has been rendered or implemented.

## What works and must stay

The approved photographic introduction gives the research question, exact pool and two explicit independent outcomes. In the 390px initial viewport, Test 1 appears around y616 and Test 2 around y687: **both are visible**, so the critique is not that the landing page hides its finding. Dusk typography/color/photo/credit are coherent. The Puzzle has honest monetary scales, all twenty real award controls, exact inspection and explicit evidence limits. Missing-record/source inspection, local ledger scrolling and focused workspaces are useful research capabilities. The motion system is already integrated and tested; it cannot by itself solve the argument's ordering.
