# First-time-visitor acceptance script

**Status: prepared, not executed with participants.** Expert inspection and automated tests cannot certify understanding. Do not fill participant answers, times or success rates without observing them.

Recruit a reader unfamiliar with the project. Use the actual release-candidate React build, not screenshots. Test desktop and mobile separately. Ask them to think aloud; do not explain the research first. Start timing at the photographic introduction and allow45–60seconds, without forcing an automatic tour.

1. Ask: “What is this study investigating?” Expected meaning: documentary reproducibility of Kubu Raya FY2022 ADD, with ecology-related performance criteria; not an environmental-impact or misconduct finding.
2. Ask: “What does the zero-rupiah gap establish?” Expected: reconstructed performance pool matches sum of published allocations; arithmetic only.
3. Ask: “Why is the second test incomplete?” Expected: approved historical assessment/selection inputs were not obtained for this study; matching totals does not derive recipients.
4. Ask: “What does ARP propose?” Expected: obtain approved inputs, independently derive identities/amounts before revealing target roster, compare and document bounded outcomes. Proposed, not historically executed.
5. Ask: “Show where you would inspect a source and its limitations.” Observe actual navigation, source dialog and ability to identify inherited transcription/no fresh PDF check.

Then ask the reader to find Policy Studio and explain its fixed-FY2022-base note; inspect one published village; open an audit export. These follow-up tasks are not part of the timed story target.
