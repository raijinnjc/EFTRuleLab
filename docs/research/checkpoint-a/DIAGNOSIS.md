## 1. What problem is communicated?

The precise research problem is the difference between **reconstructing a published fiscal total** and **independently reproducing a recipient-selection decision**. The obtained research package supports exact arithmetic for Kubu Raya's FY2022 ADD performance component and a transcription of twenty positive allocations. It does not contain the approved assessment and selection evidence needed to derive the recipient identities and amounts independently.

The defensible question is “Can the decision be reproduced **from the records obtained for this study**?” It is not “Did the government make the wrong decisions?” or “Were the recipients environmentally successful?” ADD is a district-to-village transfer, distinct from national Dana Desa.

## 2. Why does it matter for fiscal governance and ecological incentives?

Published totals make one part of public allocation inspectable. A reviewer needs a different chain of evidence to examine how assessment inputs and executed rules led to named allocations. An amount can reconcile while the decision trail remains unavailable to the study.

The ecology connection is specific: the supplied PRD records ecological/climate criteria within the FY2022 performance-assessment framework, alongside enterprise, gender-responsive development and financial governance. This makes the case relevant to the accountability of ecology-related performance incentives; it does not turn the entire ADD envelope into an environmental earmark. The study examines documentary auditability, not environmental effectiveness.

This motivation is currently weakly communicated. The app brand says EFT; the opening explanation explains an ADD allocation puzzle, but does not explain why that case matters to an ecology-related fiscal-governance audience. Existing ecological-outcome disclaimers correctly limit inference, yet are not a substitute for explaining the research motivation.

Conceptual context is listed in PRD §10 (EFT review, transparency/accountability and performance grants). Full texts/access notes beyond those supplied are unavailable; do not claim fresh literature review or imply these sources verified local assessment scores. The 2024 technical guide postdates FY2022 and is not retrospective evidence of its executed rules.

## 3. What does the research establish?

Within the inherited-transcription baseline:

- Gross ADD Rp75,897,616,000 minus five deductions totaling Rp68,920,200,000 gives residual ADD Rp6,977,416,000.
- FY2022's 7% performance share of that residual gives Rp488,419,120. It is not7% of gross ADD.
- Twenty positive amounts transcribed from the published allocation annex, each Rp24,420,956, sum to Rp488,419,120. The arithmetic gap is Rp0: **Test 1 PASS**.
- FY2021's 85/12/3 and FY2022's 75/18/7 can be compared on that same FY2022 residual. The basic reduction is redistributed 60% to formula and 40% to performance. This is a **fixed-base counterfactual**, not observed interyear spending.
- An isolated researcher-side extraction discrepancy produces an apparent Rp21,084,000 gap; restoring the source-referenced BPD value restores the historical arithmetic. This is not a government error/overpayment finding.

These calculations are reproducible in the local BigInt engine. They do not establish that primary documents were freshly inspected here. “Published source” identifies the underlying legal record; the obtained record is an inherited PRD transcription.

## 4. What cannot be independently verified?

The study did not obtain four input categories: approved historical assessment values; the actual assessed universe; implemented selection/eligibility/exception/tie/rounding rules; signed recipient-selection decisions. **Test 2 INCOMPLETE** follows from that evidence limitation; no historical decision replay has run.

No claim follows about actual payments, eligibility, fairness, ecological outcomes or absence of records within government. The 118 allocation-table rows do not establish118 assessed candidates; unlisted positive awards are not zero scores. Twenty equal published amounts are not evidence of equal assessment scores or an inferred top-twenty rule.

Original official PDFs, V4 memorandum and the full V5–V7/essay/guidebook materials are not present in the repository. Available materials are the supplied PRD/handoff, local typed evidence package, source-register excerpt, architecture/design documents and current/previous application QA. No original document was downloaded or newly inspected for this checkpoint. No missing source page, score or criterion-to-award relationship is invented.

## 5. What precisely does ARP contribute?

ARP proposes an operational review procedure: lock source/budget versions; reconcile the fiscal pool; obtain approved assessments, assessed universe and executed rules/decisions; have an independent reviewer derive identities and amounts **before seeing the target roster**; then compare both with published outputs and record a bounded outcome plus responsibility/source versions.

Its distinguishing contribution in this project is the explicit evidence contract and withheld-target order of review. It does not invent transparency, reconciliation, accountability or performance grants. It has not been executed/validated as a historical selection replay, adopted by an institution or shown to improve ecological outcomes.

MATCH requires agreement of identities **and** amounts under documented rounding; it does not prove fairness/outcomes. DISCREPANCY identifies a comparable-input mismatch requiring clarification, not culpability. INCOMPLETE stops an unsupported replay. Present historical Test 2 INCOMPLETE separately from these three conditional future-review definitions.

The public demonstrator already exposes the published ledger. Withholding is a proposed procedure for a designated independent reviewer, **not access control or a blind experiment implemented by this app**. Do not imply that a visitor who has seen the awards has performed an independent blind replay.

## 6. Why use an interactive application alongside the essay?

The essay develops the argument; this app makes its bounded evidence inspectable. A reader can select an exact fiscal input, identify its source/page limitation, inspect an individual published award, see how an isolated research extraction affects arithmetic, and distinguish a fixed-base policy comparison from history. Selecting missing evidence shows where independent replay stops; selecting an ARP step shows what a future review would require.

This is useful because the reader can inspect the reasoning and evidence boundaries rather than trust a polished headline. It is not evidence that interactivity itself improves understanding; no reader study has measured that. The application remains a research companion, not a replacement essay or a claim that competition rules permit prototype submission.
