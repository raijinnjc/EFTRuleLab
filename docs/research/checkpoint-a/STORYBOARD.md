## 03 — How could verification improve?

**Full heading**

> How could verification improve?

**Method name**

> Proposed Allocation Reproducibility Protocol (ARP)

**Insight**

> Independently derive recipients from approved inputs before revealing the published roster; then compare identities and amounts.

**Persistent qualifier**

> A proposed reviewer workflow, not a reconstruction of the 2022 administrative procedure. Historical recipient replay remains INCOMPLETE.

### One workflow, all existing steps

The current eight-step implementation groups PRD F07's underlying requirements. Preserve its detail, while making the order intelligible:

| Graphical phase | Existing steps retained | Proposed selected explanation |
|---|---|---|
|Lock and reconcile|1 rule/version;2 fiscal pool/reconciliation|“Identify the dated instrument and budget version. Check the fiscal inputs and published sum as Test 1.”|
|Obtain approved decision inputs|3 assessments;4 assessed universe;5 executed selection rules|“Approved historical inputs, universe, decision records and implemented rules are required. Stop the affected replay when they are unavailable.”|
|Derive before reveal|6 independently reconstruct recipients|“A designated independent reviewer derives identities and amounts before seeing the target roster. This public app is explaining the procedure, not conducting a blind replay.”|
|Compare and record|7 compare with ledger;8 record outcome/responsibility|“Only after derivation, compare identities and amounts; record source versions, missing evidence, reviewer and responsible office.”|

The published target roster enters **only at comparison** in this proposed review procedure. Do not draw an available assessment-to-award calculation for the historical case or add a “Run replay” control. The public award ledger remains inspectable elsewhere; withholding is a procedural proposal, not newly implemented access control.

### Conditional outcome definitions

- **MATCH:** independently derived identities and amounts agree with the published outputs under documented rounding rules. This is not proof of fairness or ecological effects.
- **DISCREPANCY:** comparable verified inputs yield a documented mismatch. Identify its record/amount and seek authoritative clarification; do not infer culpability.
- **INCOMPLETE:** necessary evidence is unavailable or insufficient. Name what is missing and stop that replay without manufacturing success/failure.

Label these “Possible outcomes of a future evidence-complete review”, not three historical observations. Separately show “Current study: historical recipient replay INCOMPLETE”.

**Default context:** the proposed derive-before-reveal principle, not a long repeated legal-source introduction. Selecting any existing step exposes its records, status and proposed responsibility. “Who would be responsible?” retains its pending-institutional-confirmation qualification; no institution/official is invented.

**Primary closing action:** “Inspect sources and study limits” →#sources. **Secondary:** “Export the audit record” →#exports; “Compare allocation rules” →#rule-shift.

**Desktop:** one bone workflow canvas with the withheld-target separation as the focal relationship, conditional outcomes and one selected context. **Mobile:** readable ordered workflow and a selected detail beneath it; full-size step alternatives/transcript remain. No tiny eight-circle-only navigation or repeated eight-panel stack.
